How this works

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. It checks passwords on Google's servers and verifies the Google and Apple identity tokens, so the session is real — this site never sees your password.

Your pantry, saved recipes and shopping list are kept in this browser under your account id, not in a database. Signing in on another device starts with an empty pantry.

Guest mode is an anonymous Firebase session: it works on this device and can't be recovered if you clear your browser data.
